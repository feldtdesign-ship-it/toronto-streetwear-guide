Scarborough · The GTA proper

Scarborough

Downtown is the storefront. Scarborough is the engine — where the food, the sound, and half the artists come from. Drive out.

The GTA in one sentence: Toronto proper is where the shops are, but Scarborough, Brampton, and Mississauga are where the sound and the food actually come from. Anyone who tells you Toronto ends at the subway map hasn't eaten well.

Weekend Markets · People selling their own work

Makers & Markets

This is the cart-and-table scene. Where people like you set up and sell what they made. Check dates before you plan around one.

Off-calendar but worth knowing: City of Craft (at The Theatre Centre) and the Toronto Dark Arts Market both run in the fall — search them before you leave. And Pedestrian Sundays in Kensington, up top, is the one that fits the cart-and-sidewalk spirit best.
